# The Visual Atlas of AI Action Paths (AAP)

The figures in this repository are designed to tell one continuous story.

Rather than functioning as independent illustrations, they collectively describe how Artificial Intelligence gradually evolves from isolated capability toward sustained participation in Human–AI Civilization.

Many readers may find it useful to browse the figures before reading the full chapters.

Together they provide a concise visual overview of the entire AAP framework.

# A Suggested Reading Strategy

Readers with limited time may find the following approach particularly effective.

1. Read **README**.
2. Browse every figure in numerical order.
3. Read **AAP-011 — The HBD Evaluation Matrix**.
4. Return to the chapters that best match your interests.

This approach provides a rapid understanding of the entire framework before exploring individual topics in greater depth.

# Closing Remark

AAP is intentionally designed as both a textual framework and a visual framework.

The chapters explain the ideas.

The figures reveal the overall structure.

Together they form a visual atlas for understanding the long-term evolution of Artificial Intelligence—from models to Action Paths, from Action Paths to organizations, and from organizations to Human–AI Civilization.
